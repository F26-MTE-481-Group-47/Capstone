# Initial Project Proposals
Our first meeting focused on the problem-definition phase of the engineering design process. At this stage, our group had not yet settled on a project. Therefore, we pitched several concepts to the attending instructors to obtain feedback on their feasibility and alignment with course requirements.
<!-- more -->

More specifically, the ideas presented included:

- **Snow-clearing device:** a device intended to facilitate snow removal.

- **Aeroponic garden:** a system designed to automate the soil-free cultivation of plants through the periodic misting of suspended roots with a nutrient-rich solution.

- **Hand tremor suppression device:** a wearable device intended to stabilize involuntary hand movements. 
  
- **Firefighting robot:** a device intended to detect and suppress localized fires in hazardous environments.

In turn, the instructors provided constructive feedback on each concept, highlighting considerations that had not yet been fully addressed in our initial proposals. More specifically, discussions focused on cost, practicality, and relevance.

Presently, our project idea has yet to be finalized. Consequently, in preparation for the upcoming Preliminary Design Presentation, we aim to further investigate our shortlist of candidate concepts before deciding on a direction.
