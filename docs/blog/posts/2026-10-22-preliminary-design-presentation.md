# Preliminary Design Presentation
Our Preliminary Design Presentation (PDP) is scheduled for today. We will meet with instructors later this morning to present our proposed design, receive feedback, and evaluate the presentations of other teams within our batch.
<!-- more -->

Since our last update, we have further refined our problem and needs statements, along with our project requirements, including objectives, constraints, and criteria. This process was informed by feedback and guidance from our instructors, helping us clarify the scope of the project and establish a more concrete foundation for the design process.

We have also researched relevant patents and prior art to identify additional design constraints and opportunities for innovation. To support effective project management, we developed a project timeline, Gantt chart, milestone list, and risk management strategies. Additionally, we created a morphological chart to explore potential design solutions, generated four distinct preliminary design concepts, and evaluated alternatives using a weighted decision matrix based on our project criteria. This process allowed us to identify the most promising candidate for further development.

Following today's presentation, we will transition into the detailed design phase. Our efforts will shift toward translating our initial concept into a technically feasible design through more rigorous engineering analysis and component-level development. This will involve refining intermediate design features, identifying and evaluating major system components, producing technical drawings and circuit diagrams, conducting relevant calculations and simulations, and interpreting the resulting analyses. We will also investigate suitable materials and components, considering their performance, availability, cost, and compatibility with the overall system.

Together, these activities will help us establish a more technically substantiated design, laying the groundwork for subsequent implementation, integration, and testing.
