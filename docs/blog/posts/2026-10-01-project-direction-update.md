# Project Direction Update
After much deliberation, our group has decided to pivot away from the project concepts presented in our initial proposals. Our current direction is now centred around the development of an autonomous cargo-carrying robot capable of following a designated user.
<!-- more -->

In particular, we have decided to focus on the challenges associated with carrying items over long distances on foot, particularly for individuals with limited mobility or carrying capacity.

We will be meeting with instructors later today to discuss this new proposal and its alignment with course requirements.
